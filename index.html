01

组织初心Our Founding Philosophy

因光而聚，薪火相传Ignited by Light, Passing the Torch

每一个改变世界的伟大的构想，在最初萌芽时往往最为脆弱。早期创办人不仅面临资金与资源的匮乏，更常常在孤立无援的摸索中陷入困境。 Every world-changing idea is often at its most vulnerable when it first begins to sprout. Early-stage founders face not only a scarcity of capital and resources, but frequently find themselves navigating deep isolation and trial without guidance.

OpeNext Foundation 的诞生，源于我们发起团队一份深刻的切身体悟——基金会的董事与核心成员们在早年创业与探索的道路上，都曾幸运地获得过行业先驱与公益力量的无私点拨与庇荫。正是这段被爱与善意照亮的经历，让团队对初创者的痛点与渴望有着骨肉相连般的深刻洞察。 The creation of the OpeNext Foundation was born from a profound personal understanding shared across our founding team. Early in their own careers and exploratory journeys, our board members and core team were fortunate enough to receive selfless guidance and shelter from industry pioneers and philanthropic forces. It is precisely this firsthand experience of being lifted up that gives our team a deep, empathetic connection to the true pain points and aspirations of early-stage founders.

怀着这份感恩与责任，发起团队汇聚多年积累的全球资源与经验，成立了 OpeNext 慈善基金会。我们致力于将这份公益温情世代传递，为全球青年创业者、第一代创办人及缺乏资源的弱势创业群体搭建一座通往未来的桥梁，用专业与爱心为他们的梦想保驾护航。 Guided by gratitude and a sense of duty, our founding team gathered years of accumulated global resources and expertise to establish the OpeNext Charity Foundation. We are committed to passing this spirit of philanthropic warmth down through generations—building a bridge to the future for young entrepreneurs, first-generation founders, and underserved communities worldwide, and safeguarding their dreams with professional dedication and care.

02

组织信念Our Core Belief

打破边界，赋能全周期成长Breaking Boundaries, Empowering Full-Lifecycle Growth

从硅谷的技术浪潮到上海的产业活力，从台北的创新韧性到东京的精益求精，OpeNext 基金会汇聚了跨越全球的顶尖学者、技术先锋与资深管理者。 From the technological waves of Silicon Valley to the industrial vitality of Shanghai, from the innovation resilience of Taipei to the commitment to excellence in Tokyo, the OpeNext Foundation unites top scholars, technology pioneers, and seasoned executives from around the globe.

我们深信，真正的公益不仅在于急难救助，更在于通过教育与知识的赋能，赋予人创造价值的长久能力。 We deeply believe that true philanthropy goes beyond emergency relief; it lies in empowering people with enduring capabilities to create lasting value through education and knowledge.

基金会秉持“打破地域壁垒，赋能企业全生命周期”的信念，构建了一个跨国界、跨领域的创新培育生态： Guided by the philosophy of “breaking geographical barriers and empowering enterprises throughout their full lifecycle,” the Foundation has built a cross-border, multi-sector ecosystem for cultivating innovation:

无界共享Boundaryless Sharing

消除地理与阶层的阻隔，让缺乏传统商业教育资源的创办人也能平等对接全球顶尖的智力支持。 We eliminate geographical and social barriers, ensuring that founders who lack access to traditional business education can equally connect with top-tier global intellectual support.

全程陪伴Full-Lifecycle Mentorship

不只停留在概念阶段，而是沿着创办人成长的轨迹，从初期的商业模式验证，到成长期的组织扩张，再到成熟期的科技升级，提供循序渐进的系统性支持。 Moving beyond mere conceptual discussion, we walk alongside founders as they grow—providing systematic, step-by-step guidance from early business model validation, through organizational scaling during growth, to technological upgrades in maturity.

  1. 初期Early商业模式验证Business model validation
  2. 成长期Growth组织扩张Organizational scaling
  3. 成熟期Maturity科技升级Technological upgrades

价值共创Co-Creating Value

以基金会为纽带，连接全球顶尖科技公司与高等学府的无偿志愿者，将点状的善意汇聚成澎湃的创新动能。 Serving as a vital nexus, the Foundation channels the pro-bono efforts of volunteers from top global tech companies and higher education institutions, transforming individual acts of goodwill into a powerful engine for global innovation.

(1)

培育创新基因Cultivating the DNA of Innovation

全周期创业陪伴Full-Lifecycle Entrepreneurial Support

我们陪伴创业者走过最艰难的“从 0 到 1”。针对初期创办人，基金会已成功帮助数十个具有社会价值的早期项目完成商业模式梳理与核心团队匹配，加速公益创新成果落地；对于探索升级的企业，我们已累计提供近百份针对性的技术升级与跨国发展建议，帮助他们突破发展瓶颈，走上可持续的高质量发展之路。 We walk alongside founders during their most challenging “0 to 1” stage. For early-stage founders, the Foundation has successfully guided dozens of socially impactful early-stage projects through business model refining and core team matching, accelerating the real-world deployment of philanthropic innovations. For growing enterprises seeking transformation, we have delivered nearly 100 tailored blueprints for technical upgrades and international expansion, helping them overcome growth bottlenecks and achieve sustainable, high-quality development.

(2)

搭建知识桥梁Building Bridges of Knowledge

跨区域思想交融Cross-Regional Exchanges

知识因分享而倍增。基金会每年在硅谷、上海、台北及东京四大核心枢纽举办 50+ 场线上线下公益研讨会与行业讲座。我们邀请全球行业领袖与高校资深学者无偿开讲，围绕前沿技术、创业经验与产业趋势展开深度碰撞。这不仅帮助世界各地的优质项目获得全球视野，也让国际顶尖的创新经验能够惠及更广泛的青年群体。 Knowledge multiplies when shared. Annually, the Foundation hosts 50+ online and in-person charitable seminars and industry lectures across our four core hubs: Silicon Valley, Shanghai, Taipei, and Tokyo. We invite global industry leaders and senior scholars to share their knowledge pro bono, engaging in deep dialogues on cutting-edge technology, entrepreneurial lessons, and industry trends. This not only provides projects worldwide with a global perspective, but also makes world-class innovation insights accessible to a broader audience of young founders.

(3)

躬身实操指导On-Site Practical Guidance

定制化公益咨询Customized Philanthropic Advisory

我们相信践行出真知。基金会定期组织无偿导师团队与创业者深入美国、日本、韩国及大中华区进行实地走访与考察调研，累计深入近百家初创团队与企业。导师们结合实际运营中的痛点开展“一对一”现场诊断，将前沿技术成果与实战经验倾囊相授，精准破解发展难题。 We believe that true insight comes from hands-on engagement. The Foundation regularly organizes pro-bono mentor teams to conduct field visits and market research alongside founders across the United States, Japan, South Korea, and the Greater China region, deeply engaging with nearly 100 early-stage teams and enterprises. Our mentors conduct on-site “one-on-one” operational diagnostics, sharing cutting-edge technological insights and practical experience to help founders overcome critical operational hurdles.

(4)

产学研协同Industry-Academia-Research Collaboration

育人育才，点亮未来Educating Talent, Lighting the Future

青年是创新的未来。基金会与全球 20+ 所顶尖高等院校及数家知名企业深度合作，设立公益助学与青年孵化项目；我们为来自斯坦福大学、伯克利加州大学等学府的青年创办人提供多轮无偿创业指导，协助优秀人才规划职业转型与创新实践，让智慧在最需要的地方生根发芽。 Youth represent the future of innovation. The Foundation collaborates closely with 20+ top global universities and world-renowned enterprises to establish charitable aid and youth incubation programs. We provide multiple rounds of pro-bono entrepreneurial mentorship to young founders from institutions like Stanford University and UC Berkeley, helping talented individuals navigate career transitions and practical innovation, allowing wisdom to take root where it is needed most.
